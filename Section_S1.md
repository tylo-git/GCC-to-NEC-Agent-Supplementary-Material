# Section S1. GCC NEC Documents Used in This Study

Table 1. GCC Document Inventory

| No. | Document Title | Issuing Authority | Role in Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | General Conditions of Contract for Building Works (1999 Edition) (gf541a) | Development Bureau (DEVB) | Baseline standard conditions for government building works; source of clauses on roles, time, payment, variations, claims, and dispute resolution under "traditional" forms. |
| G2 | General Conditions of Contract for Civil Engineering Works (1999 Edition) (gf548) | DEVB | Baseline standard conditions for civil engineering works; key reference for remeasurement arrangements, Engineer's powers, and risk allocation in traditional Hong Kong civil contracts. |
| G3 | Technical Circular – Introduction / promulgation of GCC for Building Works (1999 Edition) (e.g., WB 1900) | DEVB | Provides contextual information on policy intent, implementation arrangements, and transitional provisions for adoption of the 1999 GCC forms. |
| G4 | Project Administration Handbook for Civil Engineering Works 2024 Edition – Chapter 5: Contract Documents | CEDD | Describes the composition of standard contract documents, including how GCC is combined with specifications, drawings, BQ, and standard forms; used to understand GCC's role in the full contract package. |
| G5 | Project Administration Handbook for Civil Engineering Works 2024 Edition – Chapter 9: Measurement Procedure | CEDD | Details measurement principles, remeasurement with approximate BQ, and links between GCC measurement arrangements and NEC–ECC Hong Kong Edition Options B and D, providing an important bridge between GCC and NEC worlds. |

Table 2. NEC Document Inventory

| No. | Document Title | Issuing Authority / Author | Role in Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | Reference Materials – Frequently Asked Questions on NEC3 Collaborative Contracts (Version 1, September 2015) | Construction Industry Council (CIC) | Provides early Hong Kong-facing Q&A material on NEC3 collaborative contracting; useful for modelling local interpretive questions and adoption concerns at the NEC3 stage. |
| N2 | Practice Notes for NEC – Engineering and Construction Contract (ECC) for Public Works Projects in Hong Kong — historical 2017/NEC3-based edition | Development Bureau, HKSAR Government | Provides the historical Hong Kong implementation baseline before the later NEC4-oriented updates; useful for comparing the transition from NEC3-based public-works practice to the current NEC4 framework. |
| N3 | Practice Notes: New Engineering Contract (NEC) Engineering and Construction Contract (ECC) for Public Works Projects in Hong Kong (December 2024) | Development Bureau, HKSAR Government | Main current Hong Kong implementation guide for NEC ECC in public works; provides guidance, benchmarking, alignment of departmental practices, option selection, tender documentation, project management, risk allocation, and compensation-event administration. |
| N4 | A Practical Guide to the NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract | Michael Rowlinson / John Wiley & Sons | Provides a practical explanation of NEC4 ECC procedures, including contract data, mutual trust and cooperation, communications, early warnings, payment, cost components, compensation events, termination, and dispute resolution. |
| N5 | An Analysis of the Use and Implementation of NEC vs Traditional Forms of Contract in the HK Construction Industry | Robert Neil Dickson / University of Bath dissertation; hosted by NEC | Provides empirical and contextual material on NEC adoption in Hong Kong, including comparison with traditional forms, implementation barriers, culture, questionnaire/interview evidence, and case-study material. |
| N6 | CEDD NEC Playbook (Construction), Version 1.0, April 2025 | Civil Engineering and Development Department (CEDD), HKSAR Government | Provides operational know-how for administering NEC4 ECC in CEDD projects, especially collaboration, risk management, programme management, and compensation-event management. It should be treated as practical guidance, not as a mandatory legal/contractual authority. |
| N7 | NEC3 Construction Contracts: 100 Questions and Answers | Kelvin Hughes / Routledge | Provides NEC3-oriented Q&A explanations on contract selection, main and secondary options, roles, early warnings, compensation events, programme, payment, termination, disputes, and tendering. Useful as a practical interpretive source for NEC3 mechanisms. |
| N8 | NEC3 Engineering and Construction Contract (ECC) | NEC / Thomas Telford / ICE Publishing | Primary NEC3 ECC standard-form material; used as the baseline contract text for core clauses, secondary options, cost components, contract data, and Options A–F. |
| N9 | NEC Engineering and Construction Contract (ECC) Hong Kong Edition / NEC4 HK Edition | NEC with Hong Kong Development Bureau | Primary localized NEC4 ECC material for Hong Kong; important for analysing how standard NEC4 ECC is adapted to Hong Kong legislation, procurement practice, dispute resolution, and DEVB works policies. |
| N10 | NEC4 Managing an Engineering and Construction Contract. Volume 4: User Guide | New Engineering Contract / NEC | Official NEC4 user guidance for managing NEC4 ECC documents; supports the Agent’s understanding of procurement strategy, contract preparation, supplier selection, contract management, and contract administration. |
| N11 | Practice Notes for NEC – Term Service Contract (TSC) for Public Works Projects in Hong Kong — July 2017 | Development Bureau, HKSAR Government | Extends the Hong Kong NEC corpus beyond one-off capital works to term-service/maintenance-type procurement; useful for showing how NEC principles are generalized to long-term service delivery. |
| N12 | The Contractor’s NEC3 ECC Handbook | Steven C. Evans / John Wiley & Sons | Provides contractor-side practical interpretation of NEC3 ECC; useful for balancing government/client-side practice notes with contractor-facing administration, obligations, liabilities, and day-to-day management concerns. |
| N13 | The NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract: A Commentary 3rd ed. | Brian Eggleston / John Wiley & Sons | Provides clause-by-clause legal and interpretive commentary on NEC4 ECC; useful for legal interpretation, comparison with previous versions, and deeper analysis of main options, secondary options, risk, payment, compensation events, and other core clauses. |
